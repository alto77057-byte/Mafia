YOUR STORY STARTS HERE

ENTER THE UNDERWORLD.

One phone, one table. The phone deals secret roles, runs every night and counts the votes, so nobody has to narrate.

  1. DEAL

    Each player takes the phone in turn and taps a card to see a secret role.

  2. NIGHT

    Eyes closed. The Mafia choose a victim, the Doctor protects, the Police investigate.

  3. DAY

    The town learns who died and argues it out against a timer.

  4. VOTE

    Hands go up and one player is eliminated. Then night falls again.

The town wins when every Mafia member is gone. The Mafia win when they equal the town.

This version is for players sitting together and sharing one device. Online rooms and invite links are not included.
